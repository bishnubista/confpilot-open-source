# Code of conduct

ConfPilot is software for running conferences, so the project holds itself to the standard a good conference holds: people participate without harassment, and disagreement stays about the work.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume competence and good faith. Ask what someone meant before assuming the worst reading.
- Keep criticism aimed at code, design, and decisions rather than at people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. A rejected contribution is a scoping decision, not a judgement of the contributor.
- Respect the security process. Report vulnerabilities privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks, in the repository or in any project space.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.
- Submitting code or content you are not authorized to contribute, or deliberately introducing insecure behaviour.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel. If no private channel is available, open an issue asking for one and include no incident details, names, screenshots, or other sensitive information in that public issue.

Reports submitted through a private channel are handled confidentially. A public fallback issue is only a request to establish a private channel; it is not an incident report. Maintainers will acknowledge a private report, decide on a response, and tell the reporter the outcome. Maintainers who violate this policy are subject to the same consequences as anyone else.

Consequences are proportionate and may include a request to change behaviour, removal of a contribution, or a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from the norms established by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), rewritten for this project's scale.
